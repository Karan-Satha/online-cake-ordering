Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

This section explains how Home Cake handles personal data when you sign up through the Welcome Voucher Overlay or the Cake Club newsletter. It should be read alongside any information shown when you use other parts of the website.

Who is responsible for your data?

Home Cake, based in London, UK, is responsible for this newsletter sign-up information. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact hello@homecake.co.uk.

What information do we collect?

We collect the email address you submit. Our newsletter system also records your subscription status, sign-up source, subscription and unsubscribe timestamps, and a unique token used to process unsubscribe requests.

How do we use it?

If you opt in, we use your email address to:

  • send your WELCOME10 voucher and related welcome email;
  • send newsletters, product and seasonal updates, and promotional offers;
  • manage your subscription, record your preferences, and honour unsubscribe requests; and
  • respond to questions about your subscription.

The basis for these newsletter and marketing messages is your consent. You can choose not to sign up and can withdraw consent at any time.

Who processes the information?

Your subscription record is stored in Home Cake’s server-side database. The website does not store the newsletter record directly in your browser. We use Resend to deliver email, so Resend processes your address and the information in each message for delivery. See Resend’s Privacy Policy and its current subprocessor information.

How do we protect and retain it?

The subscriber list is accessed through the server-side application and is not exposed directly to the website page. The application uses SSL for its database connection by default when configured with the production database URL. Home Cake uses technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal data. No method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

We keep your record while you are subscribed. If you unsubscribe, the record is marked as unsubscribed and retained so we can respect your choice and avoid sending further marketing emails. Unsubscribing does not itself erase the record; contact us if you would also like to request erasure.

International processing

Resend is operated by Plus Five Five, Inc. in the United States. Email delivery may involve processing in the United States or other locations where Resend or its subprocessors operate. International transfers are subject to the safeguards required by applicable data protection law. See Resend’s privacy and subprocessor information for current details.

Your rights

Where applicable under the GDPR or UK GDPR, you may request access to or correction of your personal data, ask for erasure or restriction of processing, request a portable copy of information you provided, object to processing (including direct marketing), and withdraw consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

To unsubscribe, use the link in a marketing email. To make another privacy request, email hello@homecake.co.uk.

For information about GDPR rights, visit the European Commission’s data protection guide.
